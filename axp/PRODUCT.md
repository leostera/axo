# Product context

- **Product:** AXP, an open draft protocol (JSON-RPC 2.0, JSON Schema, ATXF logs) for moving live coding-agent Sessions between hosts, hubs and clients.
- **Surface:** the specification website at axp.leostera.dev. Mode: **Read**. Visitors are implementers of hosts, hubs and clients who need to understand and cite the spec.
- **Brief (from Leo):** a serious, RFC-style protocol site, like atxf.leostera.dev: Kumo UI, simple, RFC/RFD style.
- **Implications:** document-first pages with RFC-style front matter (designator, status, version, updated, author, conventions), numbered sections, a contents sidebar, plain tables and code. No marketing hero, no feature cards. One page for the core, one per extension, the extension registry, examples and schemas. Kumo's tokens, components and system typography; light and dark follow the system setting.
- **Assumptions (not confirmed by Leo):** the documents in `axp/` are canonical and the site only renders them; the audience is implementers rather than end users.
